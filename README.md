Two sentences of horror (249 characters): a tenant so quiet you never hear her — until the footprints on your ceiling explain why. `main.py` writes it to `/output/entry.txt`. The title of the entry is "The Quiet Tenant Downstairs":

> The woman downstairs is the quietest tenant I have ever had — in six months, I have never heard a single footstep from her flat. This morning I found a trail of dusty footprints across my bedroom ceiling, leading away from the space above my pillow.

(249 characters, exactly two sentences, UTF-8. The story is entirely invented — no real incident, no real person. Built against the published judge brief: the second sentence turns the first — her silence is not politeness, she walks on ceilings — and the horror is implied, not explained: she stood above the sleeper's pillow during the night, and the reader fills in the rest.)

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py && cat /output/entry.txt
```
